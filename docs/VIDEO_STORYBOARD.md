# Video storyboard (v2, live-action intro)

Goal: anyone understands the problem in 15 seconds, sees the real product fix it in 60, and remembers one line at the end. Total 1:55.

## Act 1: the problem (0:00 to 0:18), live action, no UI

| Time | Shot | Camera | Action | Sound | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Dark bedroom, 6:44 AM. Alarm clock glows. | Slow push-in on the clock | Clock flips to 6:45 | Alarm beep, cut short | "6:45 AM" |
| 0:02 | Marco, the HVAC tech, sits up coughing, wrapped in a blanket | Handheld, close | He grabs his phone, squints | Cough, sheets rustle | |
| 0:05 | Phone screen over his shoulder | Insert shot | He types in Slack: "Woke up with a fever, can't make it in today. Sorry!" and hits send | Keyboard taps, Slack send "whoosh" | |
| 0:08 | Small office, dawn. Dana the dispatcher, coffee in hand, sees the Slack ping | Medium shot, rack focus to her face | Her smile drops | Slack ping | |
| 0:10 | Wall board full of job cards; four cards with Marco's name light up red one by one | Fast whip to the board, then 4 quick punch-ins | Red cards: "8:15 Rosa, AC out, 94 F", "11:00 Brightside Dental, contract", "2:00 Linh", "3:10 Diego" | Four rising "error" blips | "4 customers are waiting." |
| 0:14 | Dana's hand hovers between a phone, a spreadsheet and a calendar | Top-down | She freezes | Clock ticking gets louder | "Who covers?" |
| 0:16 | Hard cut to black, then the logo types in, pixel by pixel | Static | | Single clean chime | "SECOND SHIFT: fixes the day in seconds" |

Narration over Act 1: "It's six forty-five in the morning. Marco just called in sick. Four customers are waiting for him today. Who covers his jobs? Figuring that out by hand takes an hour. Second Shift does it in seconds."

Production notes: generate each shot as a 3 to 4 second clip (fal text-to-video or image-to-video from a styled still), keep one look across shots (warm dawn light, near-black shadows, one coral accent light that matches the brand), no readable text generated inside the clips; add all text in the edit.
