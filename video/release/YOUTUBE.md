TITLE
Second Shift: the AI dispatcher that fixes a sick day in seconds (Slack + Google Calendar + Sheets + Gmail)

DESCRIPTION
It's 6:45 AM. A technician calls in sick, and four customers are waiting. Who covers?

Second Shift is an AI agent that re-plans a service crew's whole day in seconds:
1. Someone posts in Slack: "Marco is out."
2. It reads every job in Google Sheets and every tech's day in Google Calendar.
3. A solver builds a new plan: right skills, promised arrival times, no double-booking.
4. A person says yes. It updates Calendar and Sheets, messages the crew in Slack, and emails customers in Gmail.
5. It double-checks every change. And you can ask "what if Wei doesn't show up?" before it happens.

We can't predict the day. We can be ready for it.

Built for the Multi-App AI Agent Hackathon (September 13, 2026).
Code, setup, and reliability results: https://github.com/anirxdh/second-shift
